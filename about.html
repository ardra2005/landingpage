Why Puppies Are So Special

Puppies bring boundless joy and unconditional love into our lives. They are full of energy, curiosity, and cuddles. Raising a puppy requires commitment—but it’s also one of the most rewarding things you’ll ever do.

Puppy Care Considerations

  • 🛡️ **Vaccinations & Vet Checkups** – Schedule regular vet visits to ensure your puppy is healthy and protected.
  • 🏡 **Safe Environment** – Puppy-proof your home to avoid accidents with cords, chemicals, and small objects.
  • 👶 **Socialization** – Expose them to other dogs, people, and sounds early on to build confidence and reduce anxiety.
  • 🛏️ **Routine & Sleep** – Puppies need 16–20 hours of sleep per day. Create a calm space for them to rest.

Basic Training Practices

  • 🦴 **Positive Reinforcement** – Use treats and praise when they do the right thing.
  • ⏱️ **Short Sessions** – Train for 5–10 minutes at a time to avoid overloading them.
  • 🧻 **Potty Training** – Be consistent. Take your puppy out every 2–3 hours and after meals or naps.
  • 🙅 **No Punishment** – Correct gently, never yell. Redirect bad behavior instead.

Feeding Tips

  • 🥣 **Consistency is Key** – Feed at the same time every day to build routine.
  • 💧 **Always Provide Fresh Water** – Keep a clean bowl of water available 24/7.
  • ⏳ **No Free Feeding** – Avoid leaving food out all day. Stick to scheduled meals.
  • 🚫 **Avoid Human Foods** – Grapes, chocolate, onions, and caffeine are toxic to dogs.
